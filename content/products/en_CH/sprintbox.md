**SprintBox AI** is your AI-powered co-facilitator for product and innovation teams. Instead of long workshop marathons, SprintBox AI consolidates the perspectives of your stakeholders, domain experts, and testers into structured, asynchronous sprints — delivering decision-ready solutions in hours instead of weeks.

It turns unstructured input into a clear, validated outcome: fewer meetings, faster decisions, and no more expensive rework loops.

## Sprint Formats
- **Focus**: A compact sprint for a single focused question — ideal when you need clarity fast
- **Compact**: A structured sprint for teams who want to validate multiple perspectives
- **Classic**: A full format for complex innovation and product decisions

## What you get
- Prioritized problem maps drawn directly from your stakeholders' voices
- Concrete, validated solution concepts — grounded, not guessed
- Real customer feedback baked into the decision process
- Asynchronous, distributed, and remote-ready — your team works when it suits them
- Scales from a single question to a cross-functional innovation process

## Who it's for
- C-level leaders who want to lock in decisions faster
- Heads of Innovation and innovation teams
- Product Owners and cross-functional product teams
- Organizations where stakeholder coordination eats too much time today
